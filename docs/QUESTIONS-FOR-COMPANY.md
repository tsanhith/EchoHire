# Questions for the Company / Senior

*Answers to these shape the agent's prompts, question bank, and evaluation rubric. Fill in inline and commit, or answer over chat.*

## 1. Roles & job descriptions
- Which roles will this screen for? (one role first is fine — which?)
- Do you have written JDs for them? (share the actual JD text — the agent uses it to ask relevant questions)
- Minimum requirements per role (years of experience, must-have skills, education)?

## 2. The question bank
- What does HR ask **today** in a first round? (the actual list — even rough notes)
- Which questions are mandatory for every candidate? e.g.:
  - Current CTC / expected CTC
  - Notice period / earliest joining date
  - Reason for leaving last company
  - Willingness to relocate / work from office
- Any role-specific screening questions (e.g. "have you worked with X technology")?

## 3. Evaluation criteria
- What makes HR **reject** someone at this stage? (CTC out of band, notice period too long, communication, fake-sounding experience…)
- What CTC range per role is acceptable? (agent won't reveal it — used for scoring only)
- Should the agent give a recommendation (proceed/reject/borderline) or scores only?

## 4. Company pitch
- 2–3 line company introduction the agent should give candidates
- What is the agent allowed to answer about the company? (team size, work model, location, benefits) — and what must it deflect to a human?

## 5. Process
- Who reviews the results, and how do they want them? (dashboard / email / Excel export)
- Should candidates be told upfront they're talking to an AI? (**recommended: yes**)
- How soon after applying should the interview happen? (instant link vs scheduled slot)

## 6. Data & privacy
- Is it OK for resume text and interview audio to pass through third-party APIs (Deepgram, Groq — US-based)? Any candidate-data policy to respect?
- How long should we keep transcripts/recordings?

## 7. Volume
- Roughly how many candidates per week/month? (decides whether free tiers are enough — they cover ~1 interview at a time, dozens per month)
